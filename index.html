02 Vertical Multiplier Calculator

Change ADS or scope sensitivity by a small amount (~1–3 points) while keeping your existing vertical recoil-control feel. Works because PUBG sensitivity scales logarithmically: +15 sensitivity ≈ 2×. Vertical multiplier runs 50–150 in-game.
